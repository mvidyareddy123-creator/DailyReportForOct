Management report

Summary. 268 leads came in on 22 Oct. 204 (76%) have a call note, but only 57 (21%) were actual conversations; 124 (46%) were not reached, and 64 (24%) have no note at all. Only 5 leads (1.9%) are Hot or Warm, and the 49 Follow Up leads are almost all (40) from the conversations that did happen.

Key insights

  1. One in four leads is untouched, and it is concentrated. 62 of the 64 no-note leads are still in stage “Lead”. Hyderabad has 17 of 24 untouched (71%), Indore 8 of 8, and 18 leads have no branch tagged to them (Selvi K, Roopika P, Athira T V and others). Four advisors (Priyanka Divya Santh, Surekha Kashapogu, Abhinaya Sri Pendela, Selvi K) hold 28 untouched leads between them.
  2. AP/TS is the volume engine but the contact rate is weak. 144 leads (54%), but only 24 engaged (17%) and 84 not reached (58%). Vijayawada is the clearest case: 47 leads, 37 not reached, 8 engaged, and both of its FB forms are ~70% unreached. Vizag: 14 of 17 unreached. Kakinada: 21 of 42 unreached.
  3. Karnataka and Tamil Nadu convert contact into conversation better. Karnataka engaged 21 of 61 (34%), Tamil Nadu 10 of 23 (43%). Mysore (10 engaged of 33) and Bangalore (6 of 13) lead Karnataka; Coimbatore and Chennai lead Tamil Nadu. The Bangalore and Coimbatore forms engage ~50–75%, though on small volumes.
  4. Callback pile is large. 132 leads (49%) sit in Call Back and 124 notes say not reached. Advisors are mostly logging “not answered” and a WhatsApp message; few record a second attempt time.
  5. Number quality is a minor issue. 7 wrong/invalid numbers and 16 not-interested/not-a-fit (local-jobs-only, students, language). The Aug 29 forms on Hyderabad and Hubli carry the most dead weight (7–9 untouched each).

Fix first tomorrow

  • Clear the 64 untouched leads before anything else. Start with Hyderabad (17), the 18 untagged leads (assign a branch and owner) and Indore (8). Ask team leads to confirm each has a first-call note by 12 noon.
  • Re-dial the 124 not-reached leads at a different time of day and add a WhatsApp message in the same attempt. Start with Vijayawada, Vizag and Kakinada; log attempt 2 and 3 in the notes.
  • Work the 57 engaged leads for CVs and branch visits. Several have visits promised for 23 Oct (Mysore, Chennai); confirm attendance and move them out of Call Back.
  • Review the weakest forms. Vijayawada WV (Aug 29), Vijayawada Foe Number and Vizag Foe form are each ~70–75% unreached. Check number validation and call timing on them before shifting spend.
  • Where to add leads. Karnataka (Mysore, Bangalore) and Tamil Nadu (Chennai, Coimbatore, Salem) show better engagement per lead; AP/TS should get more only once its contact rate improves.
  • Notes quality. Ask advisors to move stage and write one line of intent (country, trade, experience) after every conversation; this report depends on it.
