Management report – 16 Oct 2026

Headline. 71 leads came in on Oct 16. Only 12 (16.9%) are in a live conversation (Follow Up or Warm), 29 (40.8%) are still stuck at Call Back and 19 (26.8%) have not had a single call logged. Our problem today was speed to first contact, along with lead volume.

What worked. AP/TS produced 32 leads (45%) and 8 engaged ones (25%). Vijayawada and Vizag each gave a warm lead with a firm visit plan, and Kakinada had the highest volume at 10. Tamil Nadu converted 2 of 8 into conversations. Four leads have committed to a branch visit.

Where it leaked.

  • Karnataka: 13 leads, 0 conversations. Bangalore’s 4 are all Call Back (“no response”); Mysore and Hubli lost 3 to dead/cold. This is the weakest region today.
  • 9 leads have no branch and no owner action. Assigned to System or unmapped owners, with no form source and no notes.
  • Call Back is mostly RNR. Notes show the same “not answered” repeated with a WhatsApp message and no second attempt logged.
  • Bad data. 4 wrong or invalid numbers and 2 duplicates account for 6 of 71 leads (8.5%), all lost without a conversation.
  • Stage hygiene. Nagendra Y’s two Follow Up leads read “did not answer”, one Call Back (Pranitha K) has no note, and 20 leads in total have no activity note.
  • Language. Two leads needed Hindi; one went Dead on a language barrier.
