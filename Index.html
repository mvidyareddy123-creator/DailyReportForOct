Key insights

  • Only 1.4% of leads are Warm and none are Hot. 54% sit in Call Back, 28% in Follow Up: pipeline is wide but not progressing.
  • Reachability is the bottleneck. 58% of leads carry an RNR / not-answered / busy note. Only 30% show a real conversation (CV asked, profile discussed).
  • 17 leads (8%) have no activity at all – 11 created on 8 Oct, 6 on 7 Oct. Madurai (5, all with one owner), Salem (3) and Chennai (3) lead this.
  • Re-enquiries are being missed: 7 leads re-submitted a form, yet 5 of the 7 have only unreachable notes. 40 leads (19%) are recycled, some created back in 2022.
  • Overnight demand: 93% of submissions timestamp between 9 PM and 6 AM. Verify timezone; if real, first-call timing is critical.
  • Dead leads (6) are mostly a targeting issue: age 61, 7th/10th education, no passport, local-job seekers, 1 duplicate. Hyderabad has 3 of the 6.
  • Cruise leads (26, 12%) show 62% in Call Back; Cruise Ship | Kerala has the best mix (71% Call Back, none dead/cold).

Recommended actions

  • Same-day sweep of 17 untouched leads and 7 re-enquired leads; owners to log a disposition by EOD.
  • Retry cadence for RNR: 3 attempts across 2 time slots + WhatsApp, then auto-move to Cold. Shift some calling to late evening/early morning.
  • Ask for preferred callback time: along with the WhatsApp message, ask the client for a convenient time to call and log it in the CRM. Make this a standard step for every RNR lead.
  • Pre-qualify on the form: add age, education and passport questions on Hyderabad, Vizag and Madurai forms to cut ineligible leads.
  • Standardise notes: replace free text with a disposition dropdown (RNR, Busy, CV Pending, Not Eligible, etc.).
