Management report

What happened. 292 leads arrived. 125 (43%) were taken up and 167 (57%) were not. Of the worked leads, 42 are warm or in follow up, 64 need a callback and 19 are cold or dead.

Biggest gap. Kakinada received the most leads (53) and worked 3 of them. Hyderabad (17), Vijayawada (26) and Hubli (14) also carry large untouched piles. 15 advisors hold 124 untouched leads, while 8 advisors with 5 to 10 leads each worked every one.

Unassigned. 13 leads have no branch. They sit with 6 owners, including one under “System”. These need a branch and an advisor today.

Conversion quality. The leads that were worked mostly ended in not answered. Real conversations are few: 42 leads moved to warm or follow up, and the best notes ask for a CV and record experience and passport status.

Actions for tomorrow

  1. Clear the 167 untouched leads first thing. Start with Kakinada (50), Vijayawada, Hyderabad and Hubli. Every lead gets one call and one WhatsApp, and a stage update, before new work.
  2. Rebalance Kakinada. Move part of its untouched leads to the advisors who finished every lead today, and check why the branch logged only 3 notes against 53 leads.
  3. Second-attempt rule for call backs. The 64 call backs get a second call at a different time and a WhatsApp message with a CV request, and the note records the time of each attempt.
  4. Assign the 13 unassigned leads to a branch and advisor in the morning.
  5. Note standard. No “NA”. Every note states the outcome and the next step with a date. Managers check 10 notes per branch at 4 pm.
  6. Evening check. Report the not-worked count by branch at 5 pm so it can reach zero before the day closes.
