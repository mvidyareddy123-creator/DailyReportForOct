  • These look like absence, leave or a login/assignment issue rather than slow calling – worth a check with branch heads first thing.
  • Mysore, Indore and Hyderabad are the hot spots. Mysore: 17 of 19 untouched (Bangalore, same state, has none); Indore: all 10 untouched; Hyderabad: 13 of 19 untouched. The Mysore and Hubli forms produced the most untouched leads of any source.
  • AP / TS is being worked but not reached. Vijayawada has 29 of 42 leads in Call Back – the Vijayawada forms are generating volume but first-attempt answer rates are low.
  • Nellore and Vizag are converting attention into follow-ups – best engaged share among AP/TS branches and the only place with real follow-up activity.
  • Quality signals are thin but useful: branch visits booked (Hyderabad, Nellore), resumes requested, Dussehra offer price quoted. Only 17 leads were lost, mostly duplicates, wrong numbers and "looking for local jobs".
  • 13 leads have no branch or form – nobody owns them by territory, so they stay at "Lead" (Selvi K and Aleesha hold most).
